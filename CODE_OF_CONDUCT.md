# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and critique ideas, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept feedback gracefully.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized content, and deliberate disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the project repository. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as possible.
